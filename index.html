The Bodyweight Code

  • Tension: No momentum. 3 seconds down on every eccentric movement.
  • Overload: Once the top rep range is hit, add external load via a weighted backpack the next session.
  • Rest: 90–120 seconds between sets. Do not turn lifting into cardio.

Nutrition

Protein Target: ~1g per pound of bodyweight daily.

Surplus Shake: 2 cups milk, 2 scoops peanut butter, 1 cup oats, 1 banana. Blend for ~800 clean calories.
